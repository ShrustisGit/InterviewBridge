# InterviewBridge# InterviewBridge 🎯

Interview Bridge is an AI-powered career preparation platform designed to help students improve their resume quality, interview skills, and overall placement readiness.

The platform combines AI-based analysis with industry-expert feedback to provide students with personalized guidance and measurable progress.

🚀 Key Features
📄 AI Resume Analysis – Analyzes resumes, identifies strengths and weaknesses, and provides improvement suggestions.
📊 Resume Scoring – Generates scores based on skills, projects, education, and ATS compatibility.
🤖 AI Mock Interviews – Conducts personalized interviews based on the student's resume and selected domain.
👨‍💼 Expert Mock Interviews – Allows students to receive feedback from industry professionals.
🎤 Answer Evaluation – Evaluates technical knowledge, communication, relevance, and confidence.
📝 Personalized Feedback – Provides AI-generated and expert recommendations for improvement.
📈 Progress Tracking – Tracks performance across multiple interviews and displays improvement over time.
🎯 Skill-Gap Identification – Identifies areas where students need further preparation.
🔄 AI + Human Evaluation – Combines automated AI assessment with real-world expert feedback.
